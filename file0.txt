AI (Artificial Intelligence) is technology that enables computers to perform tasks that normally require human intelligence.
AI is not truly "thinking" like a human. Most modern AI systems are very good at finding patterns and generating useful responses, 
but they do not have human consciousness, emotions, or self-awarenessu.
Examples of what AI can do:
Understand and generate language (like chatbots).
Recognize images, faces, and objects.
Translate between languages.
Help drive vehicles or control robots.
